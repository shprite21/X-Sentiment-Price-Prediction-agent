# Dataset discovery and provenance

**Decision on 17 November 2026.** Retain the existing BTC-USD assignment asset. Bitcoin is the only asset in this workspace with both a verified original Twitter archive and matching intraday prices. The financial catalogue also covers five equities, but no corresponding equity tweet file or price series was supplied locally. This selection compares available paired coverage. It does not establish that Bitcoin has the most tweets across every listed archive.

Both pages of the 21 October PDF and the public text export contain a 12-entry source catalogue. Neither contains individual posts. The newly retrieved Google Docs export matched the cached copy byte for byte (SHA-256 `7dba6d0431a3fff70736bc02fd74b855f213e9b148ec5decbab60a5280043ea`). [Assignment catalogue](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1w-Y86OjkiOjlIIth0VgTAohql9zcGi3uoDNYTaTsZuw/edit).

## Integration and evidence

**Experiment B, historical X.** Streamed 4,693,091 archive records, rejecting 3,803 malformed rows. In the 180-day window, 720,378 valid source rows qualified before sampling. The smallest seeded hashes of timestamp and text retained up to 20 distinct posts per UTC hour, giving 24,834 raw sampled records. The selection uses no prices, targets, engagement or sentiment. It discards the source-wide activity scale. Relevance filtering excluded 3,443 empty or off-topic records, and cleaned-text deduplication removed 1,148. The final set contained 20,243 scored posts. Only 1,145 of 4,248 post-warmup bars had sampled posts. [Quality ledger](historical/data_quality.json).

The provider identifies the post date as UTC. The account creation field is a different date. Display names can collide; profile favourite counts are account totals. Missing likes, reposts and views remain null. Missing original post IDs use a deterministic timestamp/text hash; the original-ID field stays null. SQL stores source, platform, original timestamp, acquisition method, license, first observation time and processing time. Raw selected rows are retained independently of normalized posts. The complete source archive is recoverable by digest. Source-specific ID records survive cross-source ingestion. Feature construction uses each canonical post once and also removes repeated cleaned text in chronological availability order.

**Experiment A, recent X.** Yahoo Finance supplied 180 days of recent prices. One authenticated Tavily basic search returned 10 articles for outlook context. Apify collection is pending, so recent X sentiment, its distribution and its incremental forecasting performance are unmeasured. See the separate [recent price-only control](current/sentiment_outlook.md) and [news provenance](current/news_context.json).

**Experiment C, historical plus recent X.** No recent X sample establishes comparable coverage or an overlapping source transition. Joining 2021 posts to 2026 prices would destroy the timestamp relationship. No cross-era performance or generalization claim is supported. The pipeline supports a combined run when source timelines overlap or meet and the intervening price series passes the calendar check.

**Experiment D, StockTwits.** A symbol-labelled, self-selected daily sample yielded 2,140 BTC.X messages aligned to 180 complete historical BTC price days under conservative date availability. Exchange-wide activity and intraday post times are unavailable. Gross/net results and validation folds are reported independently. Performance comparisons with Experiment B would conflate platform, label definition, frequency and sampling. [Supplementary evaluation](stocktwits/evaluation_report.md).
